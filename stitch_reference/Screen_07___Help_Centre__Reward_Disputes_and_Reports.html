Why was my reward flagged or escrowed? expand_more

Brand partners run automated server webhooks. If their conversion webhook takes longer than 45 minutes, our telemetry locks the reward into escrow to prevent double-charging or creator loss.

What constitutes an ambiguous trap check? expand_more

Under AdEarn Rule 2.1, attention trap questions must be deterministic (e.g., "Select the color Blue"). Questions with double negatives or debatable facts are automatically overturned in dispute.

NPCI UPI payout reversals & timeouts expand_more

If your bank's UPI switch encounters T+1 downtime, AdEarn's node retries 3 times before rolling the balance back into your creator wallet ledger automatically.

TDS deduction disputes (Section 194R) expand_more

All TDS deductions on earnings above ₹20,000/year are matched against your PAN and uploaded quarterly via Form 16A certificates on the 15th of the subsequent month.
